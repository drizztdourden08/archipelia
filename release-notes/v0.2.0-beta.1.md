# Archipelia v0.2.0-beta.1

Archipelia is a desktop app for Archipelago multiworld games: it sets up the generator for you, keeps your games, option presets and sessions in one place, and runs the room on your computer, on archipelago.gg or on a server of your own. This is the first public beta, so expect rough edges and report what you find with Report a bug in the title bar.

## New

- The Engine page sets up the Archipelago generator for you: it downloads its own Python and Archipelago 0.6.7 once, and Check again and Rebuild keep it healthy.
- The Games page installs games from the official worlds and from the community index, lists the games you have installed, and shows which of them have an update.
- Presets keep your option choices for each game, with every option described, a search over the options and a check before you save.
- A session is a saved setup: its players, each with a game, a preset and options of their own, plus the generation and server rules.
- Running a session generates the multiworld and opens its room in one step, and each run keeps its player files and its spoiler.
- A room can run on this computer, on archipelago.gg or on your own server over SSH, and the Servers page tests each saved server before you use it.
- While a room is up, the session screen shows the Players, Hints, Room, Log, Console and Spoiler widgets, and each widget can pop out into a window of its own.
- The Console sends server commands, and its Player commands menu releases or collects for one player.
- A welcome tour walks you through the engine, your first game, your first preset and your first session.

## Around the app

- Ctrl+K opens a search that finds your sessions, presets, games and servers, and runs actions such as New session or Stop the room.
- The title bar shows which room is hosting, and quitting while a room hosts asks first.
- The Storage page shows how much space each kind of data takes, clears what you no longer need, and exports or imports your library as a zip file.
- The Old runs page removes the runs you no longer need.

## Settings

- Hosting sets where new sessions run, the local port and the server rules: the hint cost, when players may release, collect and list what remains, and how long an idle room stays up.
- The archipelago.gg page keeps the private owner id of your rooms in the app's vault, opens your rooms on the site, and can forget the id.
- General sets the window mode and whether the app starts in fullscreen.

## Platforms

- Archipelia runs on Windows and Linux on x64 and on macOS on Apple silicon.
- On Windows and Linux the app updates itself, and this beta is offered only to people who turn on Include pre-releases in the updater.
- The Windows and macOS builds are not signed yet, so the system may warn you before the first start.
